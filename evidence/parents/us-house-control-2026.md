## Specific evidence

The House is the more nationalized chamber and is more sensitive to the generic ballot. Generic ballot evidence in 2026 has generally leaned Democratic by the mid-single digits in public aggregations. Democrats also appear to be prioritizing conventional swing-district nominees rather than only ideological insurgents, which may help in marginal seats.

## Baseline anchor

- Republican control remains plausible because districting, incumbency, and geographic concentration can reduce the seat conversion of a national vote advantage.
